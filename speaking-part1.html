EXAMINER
"Now, in this first part, I'd like to ask you some questions about yourself. Let's talk about your town or village. Can you describe the area where you live?"
CANDIDATE
"It's quite a small village located near the coast in the south. It's known for its peaceful atmosphere, green landscapes, and close-knit community where most residents know each other."
EXAMINER
"What do you like most about living there?"
CANDIDATE
"What I enjoy most is the tranquil environment and fresh air, especially compared to big cities. There is also a vibrant local market every weekend where people gather."
EXAMINER
"Let's talk about your accommodation. Do you live in a house or an apartment?"
CANDIDATE
"I live in a traditional two-story house with my family. It has a spacious garden in the front where we spend time outdoors during the evenings."
EXAMINER
"Which room in your house do you like the best?"
CANDIDATE
"My study room is definitely my favorite. It gets plenty of natural light, and it gives me a quiet space to focus on my coursework and projects."

SAMPLE ANSWERS & RESPONSES
Answer Key / Reference

Review the official sample answers below to compare your vocabulary, sentence structure, and fluency after trying the questions above.

Question 01: Where is your hometown located?
"My hometown is located in the northern region of the country, nestled near a scenic mountain valley. It is well-connected to nearby metropolitan areas while retaining a serene, natural charm."
Question 02: What do you like about your hometown?
"I particularly appreciate the hospitable community and the clean, pollution-free environment. Additionally, it has rich historical landmarks that attract visitors throughout the year."
Question 03: Has your hometown changed much in recent years?
"Yes, significantly. Over the past decade, improved public infrastructure, better internet connectivity, and modern educational institutions have transformed it into a thriving sub-urban hub."
Question 04: Would you recommend your hometown to visitors?
"Absolutely! Anyone interested in local culture, traditional cuisine, and scenic nature trails would thoroughly enjoy visiting my hometown."
Question 05: Do you live in a house or an apartment?
"I live in a modern apartment complex on the fifth floor. It offers great views of the city skyline and features modern amenities like high-speed elevators and 24/7 security."
Question 06: What is your favorite room in your home?
"My bedroom is my absolute favorite. It is personalized with my study desk, books, and soft lighting, making it the perfect spot for relaxing and studying."
