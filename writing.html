OFFICIAL IELTS WRITING ASSESSMENT CRITERIA
Official IELTS Guidelines

Both Academic and General Training Writing modules consist of two tasks: Task 1 and Task 2. Each task is evaluated independently by certificated IELTS examiners using detailed performance descriptors across four core criteria.

T1

Task 1 Assessment

Letter • Min. 150 words • ~20 mins
  • Task Achievement
    Assesses how fully, appropriately, accurately and relevantly the response fulfils the requirements set out in the task, covering the purpose of the letter and all required bullet points.
  • Coherence & Cohesion
    Evaluates overall organisation and logical progression of ideas, paragraphing, and varied, appropriate use of cohesive devices, connectors, and referencing.
  • Lexical Resource
    Assesses the range, precision, spelling accuracy, and appropriacy of vocabulary used for the specific communicative task.
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy
    Measures sentence variety, mix of simple and complex sentence structures, and grammatical accuracy at sentence level.
T2

Task 2 Assessment

Essay • Min. 250 words • ~40 mins
  • Task Response
    Assesses how well candidates formulate and develop a clear position in relation to the prompt, supported by evidence, well-developed ideas, and relevant examples.
  • Coherence & Cohesion
    Focuses on logical sequencing of arguments, effective paragraph grouping around central topics, and cohesive linkage between sentences.
  • Lexical Resource
    Checks for flexible use of higher-level vocabulary, awareness of collocation, natural expression, and low density of word choice/spelling errors.
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy
    Tests the candidate's control over complex grammatical structures, clause variety, tense consistency, and punctuation.

Important Marking & Scoring Principles

Weighting: The assessment of Task 2 carries more weight in overall marking than Task 1 (Task 2 contributes 66.6% of the total Writing score, whereas Task 1 contributes 33.3%).
Format Penalties: Candidate responses will be penalized if they are plagiarized or not written as full, connected text (e.g. using bullet points or note forms is not permitted).
