EXAMINER
"We've been talking about a personal possession, and now I'd like to discuss with you one or two more general questions related to this. First, let me ask about people's values. What kind of things give status to people in your country?"
CANDIDATE
"In my country, status is traditionally linked to career achievements, high educational qualifications, and modern possessions like high-end electronic devices or vehicles. However, among younger generations, intellectual accomplishments and social contribution are increasingly valued."
EXAMINER
"Have things changed since your parents’ time?"
CANDIDATE
"Significantly, yes. During my parents' era, status was primarily measured by physical assets like owning land or traditional property. Nowadays, digital skills, global exposure, and rapid career growth hold far greater importance."
EXAMINER
"Now let's discuss the role of advertising. Do you think advertising influences what people buy?"
CANDIDATE
"Undoubtedly. Modern advertising leverages targeted media and subtle psychological cues that shape consumer desires. Even when individuals believe they are making independent choices, persuasive marketing often influences their purchasing decisions."

SAMPLE ANSWERS & RESPONSES
Answer Key / Reference

Review the sample answers below to refine your vocabulary and argument structure after answering the questions above.

Question 01: What kind of things give status to people in your country?
"In my country, social status is often derived from academic qualifications, prestigious career titles, and technological assets. However, ethical leadership and social impact are also gaining strong recognition."
Question 02: Have things changed since your parents’ time?
"Yes, there has been a major shift. Previous generations prioritized physical stability like land ownership and long-term jobs. Today, young professionals prioritize digital fluency, mobility, and continuous skill development."
Question 03: Do you think advertising influences what people buy?
"Modern advertising definitely plays a powerful role. Through repetitive branding, influencer endorsements, and algorithmic targeting, advertisements make products feel desirable and essential."
Question 04: Is advertising always truthful?
"Not always. While strict advertising regulations exist, companies frequently highlight positive product features while downplaying limitations to attract buyers."
