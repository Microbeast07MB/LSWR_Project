EXAMINER
"Now, I'm going to give you a topic and I'd like you to talk about it for 1 to 2 minutes. Before you talk, you'll have one minute to think about what you're going to say. You can make some notes if you wish. Here is your topic: Describe something you own which is very important to you."
CANDIDATE
"I'd like to talk about my laptop, which is an item of immense personal and practical importance to me. I received it as a graduation gift from my parents when I enrolled in my engineering degree three years ago.

I use it virtually every single day for programming, writing technical documentation, attending online lectures, and working on collaborative team projects. What makes it so vital to me is not just its monetary value, but the fact that all my academic work, code repositories, and research files are stored inside it. Without it, maintaining my daily productivity as a student would be extremely difficult."
EXAMINER
"Thank you. Is it valuable in terms of money?"
CANDIDATE
"Monetarily, it is fairly high-end, but its true worth to me is invaluable because of the essential project data and learning resources contained within."
EXAMINER
"Would it be easy to replace?"
CANDIDATE
"While I could purchase a new hardware device, re-configuring my development environment and restoring all my local files would take considerable effort."

SAMPLE ANSWERS & RESPONSES
Answer Key / Reference

Compare your fluency, vocabulary, and response structure with the official sample answers below.

Question 01: Is it valuable in terms of money?
"Yes, financially it was quite a significant purchase. However, its emotional and functional value far exceeds its original monetary cost because of how essential it is to my daily studies."
Question 02: Would it be easy to replace?
"Buying another laptop wouldn't be difficult, but replacing all my custom software configurations, project notes, and development setups would definitely require a lot of time and effort."
Question 03: What kind of personal possessions are most popular among young people?
"Smartphones, laptops, and wireless audio devices are by far the most popular. Young people place a high premium on portable technology that enables communication, entertainment, and remote productivity."
Question 04: Why do some people hold sentimental value towards old items?
"Older items often serve as physical connections to past memories, loved ones, or significant life milestones. The sentimental value comes from the story behind the object rather than its material worth."
